## Profile

Dr. Shahzad Rasool is an Associate Professor at the [School of Interdisciplinary Engineering and Sciences (SINES)](http://www.sines.nust.edu.pk/), [National University of Science and Technology (NUST)](http://www.nust.edu.pk), since August 2016. He received his Ph.D. from [Nanyang Technological University (NTU)](http://www.ntu.edu.sg), Singapore, in 2014, his M.Sc. in [Communication Engineering](https://www.ei.tum.de/en/ei/studium/msce/) from [Technische Universität München (TUM)](https://www.tum.de/en), Germany, in 2009, and his B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from the [University of Engineering and Technology (UET)](http://www.uettaxila.edu.pk), Taxila, Pakistan, in 2006.

In 2008 he worked as a research intern at DOCOMO Communications Laboratories Europe, Munich. From 2014 to 2016 he was a Research Fellow at the [Immersive Mathematics Lab](http://www3.ntu.edu.sg/home/assourin/research) of [Fraunhofer IDM @ NTU](http://www.fraunhofer.sg), Singapore.

His recent research focuses on applications of immersive interaction. His interests include haptics, virtual reality, HCI and brain-computer interfaces.

- 🏆 Top Performer Award, NUST (2022)
- 🏆 School Best Researcher Award, SINES (2022)
- 🏆 School Best Innovator Award, SINES (2022)
- 🏆 School Best Teacher Award, SINES (2022)
